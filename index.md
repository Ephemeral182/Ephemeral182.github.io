<section class="journal service-section fade-in" id="research">
    <p class="idx">02 § Research</p>
    <h2 class="sec-title">Research <em>Interests</em></h2>
    <p class="sec-lede">I study how multimodal agents <em>execute tasks, verify outcomes, and learn from experience</em> — through code and software tools, structured evaluation, self-distillation, and persistent project state.</p>
    <div class="research-grid">
        <div class="research-card" data-color="rose">
            <div class="research-icon" data-label="Agents"><i class="fas fa-robot"></i></div>
            <h3 class="research-card-title">Multimodal Agents</h3>
            <p class="research-card-desc">Studying agents that <em>learn from interaction and sustain long-horizon work</em>. GenEvolve and DeepSearch-World explore learning from tool-use trajectories; JarvisHub provides a shared canvas and persistent project state for planning, revision, and failure recovery.</p>
            <div class="research-tags">
                <span class="tag">GenEvolve <em>NeurIPS'26</em></span>
                <span class="tag">JarvisHub</span>
                <span class="tag">DeepSearch-World</span>
                <span class="tag">JarvisIR <em>CVPR'25</em></span>
                <span class="tag">RestoreAgent <em>NeurIPS'24</em></span>
            </div>
        </div>
        <div class="research-card" data-color="violet">
            <div class="research-icon" data-label="Coding &amp; Computer Use"><i class="fas fa-code"></i></div>
            <h3 class="research-card-title">Multimodal Coding &amp; Computer-use Agents</h3>
            <p class="research-card-desc">Connecting multimodal instructions to <em>executable code and software actions</em>. Trinity explores code-driven graphic design, while JarvisVid studies video storytelling through closed-loop planning. My broader interests include agents that operate interfaces and check the results of their actions.</p>
            <div class="research-tags">
                <span class="tag">Trinity</span>
                <span class="tag">JarvisVid</span>
                <span class="tag">Computer Use</span>
            </div>
        </div>
        <div class="research-card" data-color="amber">
            <div class="research-icon" data-label="Reward"><i class="fas fa-award"></i></div>
            <h3 class="research-card-title">Reward Modeling &amp; Verification</h3>
            <p class="research-card-desc">Turning task requirements into <em>checkable criteria and evidence-grounded feedback</em>. My interests include fine-grained rubrics, multimodal judgers, reward models, and verifiers that help identify failures and guide an agent's next revision.</p>
            <div class="research-tags">
                <span class="tag">HunyuanImage 3.5 <em>Preview</em></span>
                <span class="tag">PosterReward <em>CVPR'26</em></span>
                <span class="tag">SnowMaster <em>CVPR'25</em></span>
            </div>
        </div>
        <div class="research-card" data-color="cyan">
            <div class="research-icon" data-label="Generation"><i class="fas fa-wand-magic-sparkles"></i></div>
            <h3 class="research-card-title">Visual Generation</h3>
            <p class="research-card-desc">Building visual models that <em>generate, compose, and edit</em> — from general-purpose image synthesis to poster creation, with an emphasis on visual quality, typography, layout, and alignment with human preferences.</p>
            <div class="research-tags">
