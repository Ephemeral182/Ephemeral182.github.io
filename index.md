    <p class="idx">02 § Research</p>
    <h2 class="sec-title">Research <em>Interests</em></h2>
    <p class="sec-lede">My work centers on <em>large-scale post-training, RL, and reward modeling</em> for unified multi-modal generation — and on agents that reach beyond a single model by interacting with real-world tools and visual experience.</p>
    <div class="research-grid">
        <div class="research-card" data-color="rose">
            <div class="research-icon" data-label="Agentic RL"><i class="fas fa-robot"></i></div>
            <h3 class="research-card-title">Multi-modal Agentic RL</h3>
            <p class="research-card-desc">Building multi-modal agents that <em>reason, act, and self-improve</em> via reinforcement learning over unified models — orchestrating real-world tools, references, and visual experience for grounded decision making in long-horizon visual generation tasks.</p>
            <div class="research-tags">
                <span class="tag">GenEvolve <em>arXiv'26</em></span>
                <span class="tag">JarvisIR <em>CVPR'25</em></span>
                <span class="tag">RestoreAgent <em>NeurIPS'24</em></span>
            </div>
        </div>
        <div class="research-card" data-color="violet">
            <div class="research-icon" data-label="Unified"><i class="fas fa-layer-group"></i></div>
            <h3 class="research-card-title">Unified Models &amp; Post-training</h3>
            <p class="research-card-desc">Designing unified frameworks that consolidate diverse multi-modal capabilities — local edits, global design, perception, and synthesis — into a single generalized model, and scaling them up through large-scale post-training recipes.</p>
            <div class="research-tags">
                <span class="tag">PosterOmni <em>CVPR'26</em></span>
                <span class="tag">PosterCraft <em>ICLR'26</em></span>
            </div>
        </div>
        <div class="research-card" data-color="amber">
            <div class="research-icon" data-label="Reward"><i class="fas fa-award"></i></div>
            <h3 class="research-card-title">Reward Modeling</h3>
            <p class="research-card-desc">Discovering and designing reward models that align generative outputs with human aesthetic, functional, and reasoning standards — providing robust optimization signals for RL-based post-training of foundation models.</p>
            <div class="research-tags">
